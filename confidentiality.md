# Confidentiality and publication boundaries

This repository is documentation-only.

It intentionally excludes:
- application source code;
- real company CSV files or business figures;
- employee or customer information;
- credentials, tokens, connection strings, and server details;
- internal screenshots, documents, or proprietary architecture details.

Before publishing, ensure that a high-level project description and the names of the technologies used are permitted by the applicable internship agreement and company policies. Do not add screenshots or sample data unless they are approved for public release.
