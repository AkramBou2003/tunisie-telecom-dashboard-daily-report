# Project overview

## Context
A web application was developed during a summer internship at Tunisie Telecom in summer 2025 to make selected company business indicators accessible to employees through dashboards and charts.

## Purpose
The application provides a central interface for importing daily business reports, preparing and storing report data, presenting selected indicators through visualizations, and allowing employees to consult dashboards.

## User roles

### Administrator
The administrator can manage employee accounts and use the daily report import workflow.

### Employee
Employees consult dashboards and visual reports and use the information to support their reporting activities.

## Data domains
Visualizations include indicators related to telephone recharges, mobile data, and other company activity data. No real company figures or datasets are included in this repository.
